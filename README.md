# AI-Enabled Client Service Portal

## Project overview
This project is a prototype for an AI-enabled client service management portal designed for a technology consultant role in a Microsoft-style service delivery environment. It demonstrates how service teams can combine customer support workflows, AI guidance, SLA tracking, and cross-functional issue management into a single operational platform.

## Problem statement
Client service teams often deal with high-volume support tickets, inconsistent communication, delayed issue triage, and limited access to knowledge-based recommendations. This creates operational inefficiency and slower response times for critical customer issues.

## Solution
The portal adds a streamlined experience for:
- Tracking client support requests by priority and service health
- Monitoring SLA compliance and operational performance
- Providing AI-driven recommendations for incident triage and next steps
- Creating an intake workflow for service requests and escalations
- Presenting knowledge-based guidance and operational playbooks for consultants
